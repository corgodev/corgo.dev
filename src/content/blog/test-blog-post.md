What do I want to say? How do I want it to be perceived? How do I ensure that people see what I share?

Just some of the questions that I assume every author has in their head. And just a drop in the ocean of uncertainty in my head.

I think what I miss is the "I am a creator" mindset, rather than "I want to be a creator" one.

In the case of the first one, the thing seems super simple - whatever I do makes me a creator since it's a part of how I see myself.
A random doodle? A short story that is kind of shit? A small game that took me a few hours? All proof that I am who I think I am.

In the latter case the bar is ever-moving and pushed forward by questions such as "Is it good enough?" or "Will people like it?".

And so, I decided to try and fight that. I am a creator. I am a game developer. I am a writer. Even if, by some relative measures, a shitty one for now - at least I am, what I want to be.
