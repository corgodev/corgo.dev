Last weekend, I participated in my first game jam and managed to create a complete game in just 48 hours. Here's what I learned about rapid game development, scope management, and the importance of good sleep (or lack thereof).

## The Challenge

The theme was "Reverse" and I had to come up with a concept, design, code, and polish a game in two days.

## What Went Right

- Simple mechanics that were fun immediately
- Using familiar tools (Unity + C#)
- Cutting features aggressively

## What Went Wrong

- Underestimated art time
- Skipped playtesting until too late
- Forgot to eat properly

## Key Takeaways

Game jams are incredible learning experiences. The time pressure forces you to focus on what really matters and strip away unnecessary complexity.
