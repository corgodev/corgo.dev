## How to start

I haven't talked to many blog authors but I can imagine that many of them suffer from the same issue as me - what do I start blogging about?
It can be a daunting task, imagining how to start your big new career as a blog writer.


So for my first one I chose to talk about something important that probably a lot of people can resonate with - should I leave my job?

### Rosy beginnings

It started well as it often does. New job, new colleges, new tech stack and a completly fresh beginning. Every job I had there was always something that didn't fit me - either a consultancy job that cared more about getting paid than about client's product, or a big corpo job where tech decisions were made based on hype and politics, rather than what engineers actually needed.

This time it was supposed to be different - small company, tech-first, interview process that allowed me to meet engineering director and understand how he wants to develop the company.
